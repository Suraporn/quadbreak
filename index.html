How it was tested

12-condition cost battery

Every result was re-run under different spreads, commissions and execution delays. The figures on this page use the weakest one.

Out-of-sample periods

Settings were fixed on Jan–Jun 2026 only, then judged on 2025 and Jul–Nov 2026.

Lookahead and random-twin tests

Each strategy was checked for accidental use of future data and compared against randomized copies of itself.

Independent re-implementation

Key results were reproduced by a second, independent implementation before they were trusted.

FAQ

Is QuadBreak trading live?

Yes. v1.60 is running on a live account as a forward test. The charts on this page are backtests; ask by email for the latest forward-test figures.

Do you manage my money?

No. QuadBreak runs on your own MetaTrader 5 account with your own broker. Your funds stay in your account, and you can pause or remove the EA at any time.

What do I need to run it?

An MT5 account with a broker that offers XAUUSD, ideally with low spreads and commissions, and a Windows VPS so it can run without interruption.

Which risk profile should I use?

Start with Low or Medium. High is the setting with the biggest returns in the backtest, but it also went through a drawdown of about 50%, which most people find very hard to sit through.

Can I see the strategies?

No. The logic and parameters stay private. You get the compiled EA, the risk settings and the results.

Why does my broker matter?

Spreads, commissions and execution speed change results. QuadBreak's figures already use the weakest of four cost conditions, but a high-cost broker will still do worse.
